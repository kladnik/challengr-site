# Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026.

Challengr does not collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

## Where your data lives

- **On your devices.** Your challenges, tasks, logs and notes are stored on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
- **In your iCloud.** If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your data between your devices through your own private iCloud database. Only you can access it. The developer can't see it. Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers iCloud.
- **In backups you make.** If you export a backup, the app creates a JSON file and saves it wherever you choose.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Widgets and watch complications read the same data on your device.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app from each device. To also remove the iCloud copy, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and manage storage for Challengr.

## Children

Challengr doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
