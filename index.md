# Challengr

Run your own 75-day challenge on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

## Get help

Found a bug or have a question? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/kladnik/challengr-site/issues). Please include your device and iOS version.

## Common questions

**My data doesn't show up on my other device.**
Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices, and make sure iCloud Drive is on. The first sync can take a minute.

**How do I use the Apple Watch app?**
Install Challengr on your iPhone, then open the Watch app on your iPhone and install Challengr from the Available Apps list.

**What happens on a day I'm sick or traveling?**
Open Challenge, tap Excuse days, and choose which tasks to pause. Excused days don't count as missed. You can add them to the end of the challenge.

**How do I move my data to a new phone?**
iCloud sync moves it for you. You can also export a backup in Challenge > Settings and import it on the new phone.

## Privacy

Challengr collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy).
